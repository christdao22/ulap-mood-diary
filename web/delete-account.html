Delete your Ulap account and data

You can delete your Ulap account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email. You don't need to reinstall the app to ask.

In the app

  1. Open Ulap and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and all data.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm. This needs an internet connection.

Your account and every check-in are erased from Ulap's server right away, and Ulap is cleared on your phone.

If Ulap asks you to sign in again first, that's so it knows it's you. Until you do, it can only delete the diary on that phone, and your backup stays on the server. You can also ask by email, below.

Without the app

Email privacy@ulap.app from the email address on your Ulap account, with the subject Delete my Ulap account.

We'll confirm it's you, delete your account and all its data within 7 days, and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

Your account, your sign-in details and sessions, your settings, and every check-in, including journal notes.

What's kept, and for how long

DataHow long
Encrypted server backups made before you deletedDeleted within 30 days
Crash reports (never hold notes, names or emails)Deleted within 90 days
Anything elseNothing is kept

No account?

If you never signed in, your diary is only on your phone and Ulap's server has nothing of yours. To delete it, use Account → Delete account and all data, or remove the app or clear its data in Android settings.
